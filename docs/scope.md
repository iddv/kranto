# What Kranto will not become

Kranto does three things for one person on one machine: it reads the sources you pick, keeps
a brief and idea cards from what it read, and drafts a post you approve. The plan is to do
those three well, then do the same three on more machines. This page lists what is out, so
that a "no" to a request is a link and not an argument.

## Out of scope

- **Building software.** Kranto once built small programs from ideas. That feature left;
  [archive.md](archive.md) says what it kept on your machine.
- **Investing and trading.** Also a feature that left.
- **Teams and shared installs.** One install is one person's.
- **A hosted Kranto.** It runs on your machine, under your own model key. There is no service
  to sign up for.
- **A shop of add-ons.** Cubs (the small programs that write and send posts) are an interface
  for people who write their own, not a marketplace.
- **Windows.**
- **A mobile app.** The page works on a phone for reading and approving; there is no app.
- **Reading Reddit without your own approved app.** Reddit requires its approval for any app
  that reads its data. Kranto ships no Reddit key and asks for no approval of its own.
- **Reading behind a login you did not give.** Kranto reads public sources, and accounts you
  connected yourself, read-only. Nothing else.
- **Posting without a person's click.** Every send waits on the page until you click Send.
  This does not change.
- **A scheduler or calendar for posts.** Kranto drafts and hands a post to one place you
  connected. Planning posts across many networks is another kind of tool.

## Next

- **macOS.** Today only the watching works there; the console and posting need Linux. macOS is
  the first thing on the list, and the largest.
- **Local models.** Setup accepts "another OpenAI-compatible endpoint", which is how most local
  model servers speak. Nobody has tested Kranto that way yet. Treat it as untested.
