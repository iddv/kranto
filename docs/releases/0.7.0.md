## What 0.7.0 is

The first release of Kranto. It runs on your own Linux machine as one page in your browser, on
127.0.0.1: it reads the sources you pick for your topics, keeps the few items that matter with
their links, writes a short cited brief a day and idea cards about what the news suggests, and
drafts the post about it (your blog, a newsletter, short posts for Bluesky, Mastodon, X, Threads
and LinkedIn) in your voice. **Nothing is posted until you say yes.** Your own Claude Code can
work beside every page as the console.

It needs Linux, Python 3.12, [uv](https://docs.astral.sh/uv/) and one model for reading (an
Anthropic or OpenRouter key, or your Claude Code login). Install it with the lines under
**Install** below, then follow
[docs/setup.md](https://github.com/iddv/kranto/blob/v0.7.0/docs/setup.md) from step 3; the whole
list of what is in it, what it is not, and what is unproven is in
[CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/iddv/kranto/blob/v0.7.0/CHANGELOG.md).

An install made before the rename updates to this release from the nightly build that crossed it
over (`kranto update`); its data, settings, connections and service are kept, and
`kranto update --rollback` goes back.
