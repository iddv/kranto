# Blog posts

Kranto's blog writer. Choose **Write a post** on an idea card or a news item (or ask the console to
write one), and a blog post about it waits for you on **Post**: its title, an excerpt, three to
five hundred words in your voice, and the card's or the item's own sources linked under it.

- **Copy** or **Download** it as markdown: no account needed.
- With a **Sanity** account connected (Settings › Posting), approving it files the post in Sanity
  as a draft. Kranto never publishes: you do, in Studio.
- Nothing is sent until you choose Send, and an approval in practice files nothing.

It can also write a post about this week's news (every item a brief's item, verbatim, with an
opening in your voice) or keep your own markdown as a draft.

**Your voice.** The post is written through Kranto's prose engine (docs/prose.md):
your voice card and your closest samples, Kranto's floor against the shapes that read like AI, and
the blog's template. Give it your own line ("What do you think?", optional) and the post builds
on it; without one it reports what the sources say and never speaks for you. Lines that still
read like AI are rewritten, and only those.

**Settings:** your site's name (Settings › Posting), tags every post carries, and how many days
of briefs a week's post reads. The old `blog.tone` became your voice card's first line.

Each post costs one reason-tier model call (about $0.03), and a short second call when a line
needs fixing; your own markdown costs nothing.
