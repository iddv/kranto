# A Substack post

- **The thesis in the first 50 words.**
- **A subhead every 300 to 400 words,** in sentence case.
- **One argument in depth,** with the sources' numbers and quotes, rather than a survey.
- **A Note that announces the post** is one or two sentences and the link.
- The length figures (about 1,200 to 2,000 words) are unverified defaults; change them.
