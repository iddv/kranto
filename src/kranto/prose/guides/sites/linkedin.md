# A LinkedIn post

- **A first line under 140 characters that stands alone:** it is all that shows before "see
  more". Make it the angle's claim, prediction or question. Not: "I wasn't going to post this,
  but…"
- **Then short paragraphs that back it,** as many as the argument needs, then the link.
- **Hashtags:** at most three, on the last line, or none.
- Not: "I'm thrilled to announce", emoji bullets, a question added only to draw comments.
- These defaults come from analytics firms' posts that were not verified; change any number.
