# A blog post

- **Short: 120 to 200 words by default** (the person's length switch wins), never padded: room
  to argue the angle once, and no more.
- **The first sentence is the angle's claim, prediction or question.** The reader decides there
  whether to go on. Not: "Amazon FAR's Dex-Hands team has shown a system called Pick Anything."
  Do: "Teleoperation was supposed to be the price of a robot hand that can grab anything.
  Amazon's team says it skipped it."
- **No headings in a short post.** Past about 400 words, a `##` heading names what its part
  holds, in sentence case, where the argument turns. Never "## What happened", "## Why it
  matters" or "## What we don't know".
- **Shape it to the angle,** not to a template: one argument told as a story, with paragraphs
  of different lengths. Two posts in a row should not have the same outline.
- **Cite a source where its fact is used,** as `[n]` with the numbers given. Kranto adds the
  sources list under the post.
- **End on a fact, a prediction or a question a reader could answer.** Not: a summary paragraph
  that repeats the post.
