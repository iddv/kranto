# A Mastodon post

- **Open on the reader's interest,** then the fact that answers it. Not: "LiberAI announced
  Liber-0, pretrained on over 2.2 million hours of human video." Do: "Want a robot that learns
  to grab things by watching people? LiberAI says Liber-0 does it on a third of tasks it never
  saw."
- **One post that reads well without the link,** with the angle's claim, prediction or question
  in it. A list of facts is not a post.
- **Tags:** none unless the person allows them; then one to three in CamelCase, so screen
  readers say them (#OpenSource), on the last line.
- **Conversational and plain.** People here dislike marketing voice. Not: "Huge news! 🚀"
- **The link goes last,** on its own line.
